Questions

Questions we get every day

Are your prices really fixed?

Yes, for the scope shown. If your file needs more, such as many more transactions or years of missing records, we tell you the new fee in writing before we start.

My company made no money. Do I still need corporate tax?

Yes. Every UAE company must file an annual corporate tax return, even with zero revenue. Small Business Relief can bring the tax to zero, but the return is still due.

When is my corporate tax return due?

Nine months after your financial year ends. For a December year-end, the 2026 return is due by 30 September 2027.

Is Small Business Relief still available?

Yes. It has been extended to tax periods ending on or before 31 December 2029, for revenue up to AED 3 million. You have to elect it in your return.

Do your prices include VAT?

No. Our fees are subject to 5% VAT. The FTA itself charges nothing for filing.

Can you help if I'm already late?

Yes. We bring overdue returns and records up to date and, where there are grounds, apply to the FTA to reconsider penalties.

What do I need to send you?

For most files: your trade licence, the owner's Emirates ID or passport, bank statements for the period, sales and purchase invoices, and any previous returns. We send an exact checklist after your first message.

Do I need e-invoicing?

Soon, yes. Businesses with revenue under AED 50 million must appoint an accredited e-invoicing provider by 31 March 2027 and issue e-invoices from 1 July 2027. Larger businesses start on 1 January 2027. We tell you what it means for your invoicing.

How long must I keep my records?

Seven years for corporate tax, five for VAT, and fifteen for real estate records, counted from the end of the tax period. Our bookkeeping packages keep them organised and ready for an FTA audit.
