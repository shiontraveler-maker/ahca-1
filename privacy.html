Privacy policy

Last updated 31 October 2026

AHCA is an FTA-registered tax agent (TAAN 20000204, trade licence 1618350) serving businesses in Dubai, Abu Dhabi and across the UAE. This page explains what information we collect when you use tax.ahca.ae or contact us, why we need it, and how we look after it.

What we collect

  • What you send us. Your name, company, phone number and the details of your request when you message us on WhatsApp, call or email us.
  • Documents for your file. Once you engage us, the records needed for the work, such as your trade licence, owner ID or passport, bank statements, invoices and past returns.
  • How the site is used. Basic information sent by your browser, such as pages visited, device type and approximate location. We also note which advertisement brought you to us, using a short reference code that is added to your WhatsApp message.

Why we use it

  • To answer your enquiry and send you a price.
  • To prepare and file your registrations, returns and requests with the Federal Tax Authority, and to keep your records as UAE tax law requires.
  • To understand which of our advertisements bring useful enquiries, so we spend less on the ones that do not.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for anything unrelated to your enquiry or our work for you.

Who we share it with

  • The Federal Tax Authority, when we file or correspond on your behalf.
  • WhatsApp (Meta), which carries the messages you choose to send us there.
  • Google, which serves the fonts on this site and provides the advertising and measurement tags that tell us which advertisements bring enquiries. These tags may set cookies in your browser.
  • Other professionals only when your work needs them and you have agreed.

Cookies and your browser

This site remembers your language choice in your browser so the page opens in the language you picked. Google's measurement tags may set cookies to connect a visit with the advertisement that led to it. You can clear or block cookies in your browser settings at any time, and the site will still work.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not become an engagement are deleted within 24 months. Records for clients are kept for as long as UAE tax law requires: at least five years for VAT and seven years for corporate tax, counted from the end of the tax period, and longer for real estate records.

Your rights

Under the UAE Personal Data Protection Law (Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021), you can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, and ask us to delete it where the law does not require us to keep it. Send your request to the email below and we will reply within 30 days.

How we protect it

Client files are kept in access-controlled storage and are only seen by the people working on them. We ask that you send documents only through the channels we give you.

Contact

Email abdelfattah@ahca.ae or call or WhatsApp +971 54 505 0689. Offices in Dubai and Abu Dhabi.

AHCA is a private firm and is not affiliated with the Federal Tax Authority. The FTA charges nothing for registration or filing; our fees are for preparing and handling your work.
